Dolma recipe

Description

Dolma is a traditional Azerbaijani dish made by stuffing vegetables or leaves—most commonly grape leaves—with a flavorful mixture of ground meat, rice, herbs, and spices. It’s known for its rich, tangy taste and is often served with yogurt.

Ingredients

  • Grape leaves (fresh or jarred)
  • Ground lamb or beef (or a mix)
  • Rice (uncooked or slightly rinsed)
  • Onion (finely chopped)
  • Fresh dill
  • Fresh parsley
  • Fresh mint
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Butter or oil
  • Tomato (chopped or a little paste)
  • Sumac or a splash of lemon juice (for tanginess)
  • Garlic
  • Plain yogurt or garlic yogurt sauce

Steps to prepare:

  1. Prepare filling: Mix ground lamb or beef with rice, chopped onions, fresh herbs (like dill, mint, parsley), salt, and pepper.
  2. Prepare leaves: Rinse grape leaves and soften them in hot water if needed.
  3. Stuff: Place a small amount of filling on each leaf, fold the sides, and roll tightly
  4. Arrange in pot: Line a pot with extra leaves and place the rolls snugly inside.
  5. Cook: Add a little water or broth, cover, and simmer on low heat for about 40–60 minutes.
