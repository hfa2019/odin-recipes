Dushbara recipe

Description

Dushbara is a traditional Azerbaijani soup made with tiny meat-filled dumplings served in a clear broth. The dumplings are usually smaller than typical ravioli, and the soup is light, aromatic, and comforting. It is a festive dish, often prepared for special occasions and family gatherings. A touch of vinegar or lemon juice is sometimes added before serving to enhance the flavor.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • About 1/2 cup water (adjust to make a firm, elastic dough)
  • For the Filling:
  • 200–250 g ground lamb or beef (or a mix)
  • 1 small onion, finely grated
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Optional: a pinch of cumin or sumac
  • 6 cups water or light broth
  • Salt to taste
  • 1–2 tbsp dried mint or fresh herbs (optional, for garnish)
  • Optional: vinegar or lemon juice for serving

Steps to prepare

  1. Prepare the Dough:
  2. Mix flour, egg, and salt in a bowl.
  3. Gradually add water and knead to form a smooth, firm dough.
  4. Cover and let it rest for 20–30 minutes.
  5. In a bowl, combine ground meat, grated onion, salt, pepper, and optional spices.
  6. Mix well until uniform.
  7. Roll and Shape Dumplings:
  8. Roll out the dough thinly (about 1–2 mm).
  9. Cut into small squares (about 2–3 cm).
  10. Place a tiny amount of filling in the center of each square.
  11. Fold the square into a triangle or press edges together to seal tightly. The dumplings should be very small.
  12. Cook the Soup:
  13. Bring water or broth to a boil in a large pot.
  14. Carefully drop the dumplings into the boiling liquid.
  15. Stir gently to prevent sticking.
  16. Cook for about 5–7 minutes, until dumplings float and are cooked through.
  17. Serve:
  18. Season with additional salt if needed.
  19. Optionally, add a pinch of dried mint or fresh herbs.
  20. Serve hot with a splash of vinegar or lemon juice, if desired.
